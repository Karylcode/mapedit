# Built-in material image sources

All bundled images are unmodified 1K JPEG diffuse maps from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com). Poly Haven publishes its assets under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Download metadata and MD5 checksums are recorded in `sources.json`; no network connection is required at runtime. Downloaded 2026-10-05.

Repeat widths come from the asset API's `dimensions` field (millimetres converted to metres), rounded to the stated precision. `dark_wood` uses the same wood image with a dark PBR base-color factor. White, red and blue are plain material colors with no image. UVs are generated by orthonormal projection of metre coordinates onto each triangle plane (including slopes); Agents do not supply UVs.
